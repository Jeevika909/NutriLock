Understanding your results

What does a reference range mean?
A reference range is a comparison interval supplied by a laboratory. It can vary by lab, testing method, age, and other factors. A result outside the interval does not automatically mean something is wrong, and a result inside it does not guarantee that everything is fine.
About this section
This tool organizes values you enter and compares them only with the range you provide. It does not diagnose conditions or replace the interpretation of a qualified healthcare professional.

Food explorer

Foods offer different combinations of nutrients. Eating a variety of foods helps you get different things your body needs.

🍚 Grains & cereals Rice, oats, wheat, millets and bread provide energy and may supply fibre and other nutrients.
🥜 Protein-rich foods Beans, lentils, eggs, tofu, nuts, dairy and fish provide protein for growth and everyday body functions.
🥬 Vegetables Different vegetables provide fibre, vitamins, minerals and other beneficial plant compounds.
🍊 Fruits Fruits contain a variety of vitamins, water, fibre and naturally occurring carbohydrates.
🥛 Dairy & alternatives Milk, curd, paneer and suitable fortified alternatives can provide calcium, protein and other nutrients.
🥑 Fats & oils Nuts, seeds, oils and other fat-containing foods provide energy and help the body absorb certain vitamins.

Food knowledge

A little knowledge about what food provides and how nutrients support everyday body functions.

Iron Helps the body make hemoglobin, which carries oxygen.

Sources: lentils, beans, leafy vegetables, fortified cereals.

Calcium Supports bones, teeth, muscles and nerve function.

Sources: milk, curd, paneer, calcium-set tofu.

Protein Used for growth, repair and many body functions.

Sources: pulses, eggs, dairy, soy, nuts.

Fibre Supports digestion and helps maintain normal bowel function.

Sources: fruits, vegetables, beans, oats, whole grains.

Vitamin C Supports connective tissue, healing and iron absorption.

Sources: guava, oranges, amla, peppers.

Healthy fats Provide energy and help absorb certain vitamins.

Sources: nuts, seeds, groundnut, oils.
